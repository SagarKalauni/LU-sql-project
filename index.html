🦁 The Missing Golden Lion Trophy

A university trophy has gone missing, and campus security needs your help.

The Golden Lion Trophy was last seen at Lindenwood University on October 18, 2025. The trophy disappeared sometime during the evening from Spellmann Campus Center, but security does not know exactly when or who took it.

Unfortunately, the original incident report has been misplaced.
You remember only three things:

• The Incident: Theft of the Golden Lion Trophy (type = 'theft')
• Date: Sometime on October 18, 2025 (date = 20251018)
