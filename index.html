The Missing Golden Lion Trophy

The Golden Lion Trophy was last seen at Lindenwood University on October 18, 2025. The trophy disappeared sometime during the evening, but security does not know exactly when or who took it.

Unfortunately, the original incident report has been misplaced.
You remember only three things:

• Date: October 18, 2025 (20251018)
• Location: Lindenwood University (city = 'Lindenwood')
• Incident: Missing Golden Lion Trophy

Your Mission: Retrieve the incident report from crime_scene_report, follow the evidence through the database, and discover who took the Golden Lion Trophy!
